The Challenge

The Onboarding & Bootstrapping Bottleneck

STEM students, developers, and lab administrators waste days configuring environments and risk data loss partitioning drives.

🚨 Disk Partitioning Fear
Users are terrified of resizing hard drives. Manual partition mistakes often lead to primary OS corruption and total data loss.
⏰ Syllabus Setup Friction
Installing toolchains (Matlab, Docker, Python) and resolving path variables wastes up to 40 hours of productivity at the start of a semester.
📉 Fragmented WAN Downloads
Downloading 5GB+ ISOs over unstable networks leads to failed downloads and corrupted payloads, requiring repeated retries.
The Architecture

OSwitch Product Ecosystem

A unified desktop application that automates disk manipulation, downloads, and workspace provisioning.

📱 Safe Partitioning Engine
Automated drive shrinking protected by a 3-layer Safety Guard. Users allocate partition space securely using an Apple-style slider.
📦 Master Infinite Store
Pre-verified catalog of 10,590+ tools. Students select their syllabus or engineering branch, and OSwitch installs all relevant packages in 1 click.
⚡ 8-Stream Parallel Accelerator
Spawns 8 simultaneous download chunks via HTTP Range requests. Integrates real-time stream-computed SHA256 integrity verification.
🎛️ Unified Control Panel
Real-time disk volume analytics, 1-click boot selection, and a clean OS uninstallation tool that instantly reclaims disk storage.
